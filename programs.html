Ages 4–18 Curriculum

Age-Based Football Pathways

Every age group follows clear technical, physical, and tactical milestones tailored to how young players learn and compete.

Ages 4–6 • 1x / Week (45 mins)

Little Strikers

Playful introduction to football focusing on balance, coordination, and falling in love with the ball.

  • Objectives: Confidence & joyful ball familiarity
  • Technical: Toe taps, dribbling & basic shooting
  • Physical: Agility, balance & motor coordination
  • Tactical: Spatial awareness & 3v3 fun games
Enroll in Little Strikers
Ages 7–9 • 2x / Week (60 mins)

Junior Footballers

Building core ball mastery, confident passing, and teamwork in structured 5v5 and 7v7 formats.

  • Objectives: Ball comfort under light pressure
  • Technical: First touch, short passing & 1v1 turns
  • Physical: Footwork speed & reaction drills
  • Tactical: Basic attack/defense transitions
Enroll in Junior Footballers
Ages 10–12 • 2–3x / Week (75 mins)

Youth Development

Developing positional intelligence, combination play, and composed decision-making in 9v9 football.

  • Objectives: Smarter off-ball movement & vision
  • Technical: Driven passes, crossing & finishing
  • Physical: Acceleration, stamina & core stability
  • Tactical: Width, depth & pressing triggers
Enroll in Youth Development
Ages 13–15 • 3x / Week (90 mins)

Advanced Academy

Transitioning to full-sided 11v11 football with tactical unit training and competitive league fixtures.

  • Objectives: 11v11 role mastery & game tempo
  • Technical: Tight-space combinations & weak foot
  • Physical: Sprint mechanics & injury prevention
  • Tactical: 4-3-3 / 4-2-3-1 shape & set pieces
Enroll in Advanced Academy
Ages 16–18 • 4x / Week (90 mins)

Elite Performance

High-intensity academy training for senior youth players targeting competitive showcase tournaments.

  • Objectives: Match-ready intensity & leadership
  • Technical: High-speed execution & clinical finishing
  • Physical: Explosive power, VO2 & recovery
  • Tactical: Match analysis & pressing systems
Enroll in Elite Performance
Ages 8–18 • 1–2x / Week (60 mins)

Goalkeeper Specialist

Dedicated shot-stopping, handling, footwork, and distribution coaching for aspiring academy goalkeepers.

  • Objectives: Command of penalty box & composure
  • Technical: Catching, diving form & distribution
  • Physical: Lateral plyometrics & reaction speed
  • Tactical: Angle narrowing & back-line communication

4-Pillar Methodology

How Our Academy Trains Players

Every training week follows a balanced, athletic progression from individual ball mastery to full-team matchplay.

01

Trial Assessment

Your child joins an age-matched trial session so coaches can place them in the ideal training group.

02

Technical Pitch Drills

High-repetition ball control, receiving on the half-turn, dribbling, and passing accuracy drills.

03

Speed & Conditioning

Age-safe agility ladders, sprint mechanics, balance work, and football-specific endurance.

04

Weekend Matchplay

Apply training skills in competitive academy friendlies and youth cups tracked in the Parent Dashboard.
