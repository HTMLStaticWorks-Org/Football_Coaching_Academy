Matchday Guidelines

What to Expect on Matchday

Clear matchday routines that help players feel prepared, confident, and excited to compete.

Arrive 30 Mins Early

Squad check-in, dynamic warm-up, and tactical team talk before kick-off.

Full Academy Kit

Players wear academy jersey, shin guards, football boots, and bring a water bottle.

Positive Sidelines

Parents cheer effort and teamwork while coaches guide tactical decisions.

Live Fixture Updates

Kick-off times, pitch numbers, and opponent details are posted in the Parent Dashboard.
